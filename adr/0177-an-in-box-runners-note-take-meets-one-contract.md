# ADR-0177: An in-box runner's note take meets one contract

- **Status:** accepted
- **Authority:** `internal/sandbox/executor`
- **Enforced-by:** table tests over the executor's registered runners (`Registered()`), each runner supplying its own fixture, one failing a runner that reads notes and cannot confirm: `internal/sandbox/executor.TestEveryRunnerThatTakesNotesSaysSoAndMeetsTheContract`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestANoteServedIsConfirmedOffItsJobsOwnRecord`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestARealBoxsNotesReachTheBridgesRoute`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestAWakeHandedToARunReadsBackAsAWake`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestEveryHarnessThatReadsNotesCanConfirmATake`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestATakeIsConfirmedOffTheSessionRecordThroughTheReceipts`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestATakeIsConfirmedByItsItemsDigestOffARecordOfText`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestATrailingNoteNoReplyFollowedIsHeldOnlyWhereAResumeHandsItBack`, `internal/sandbox.TestAPollServeASessionRecordHoldsIsConfirmedThroughTheReceipts`, `internal/sandbox.TestAWakeServedOnceSettlesAtTheRunsEndOffItsRecord`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestTheStandingInstructionIsGivenExactlyWhenARunReadsNotes`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestTheStandingInstructionQuotesTheHeadingANoteOpensWith`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestTheStandingInstructionQuotesTheHeadingAWakeOpensWith`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestAWakeIsAnEntryOfItsOwnAndAHeadingInsideItsFenceHeadsNothing`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestAHookLineNamingWakesIsAWakeEntry`, `internal/sandbox/executor.TestARunWiredToNotesOnAClaudeCodeOlderThanItsFloorIsRefused`, `internal/engine.TestEveryRunnersCeilingHoldsTheLargestItem`
- **Measured:** Claude Code 2.1.263 to 2.1.330 and OpenCode 2 (2.3.2 for the confirm and the poll ceiling) against a stub model API: an error answer made Claude Code re-initialise the whole bridge, a 503 was gone past, a late answer is discarded at the hook's timeout, and where a note lands differs by model (docs/concepts/code-sandbox.md, "Claude Code's hooks", "OpenCode's notes plugin"). A widest note renders at 4,493 UTF-16 units, the item bound beside it 4,505.
- **Cost-when-tried:** Claude Code before 2.1.163 ran both hooks and dropped the Stop answer (measured on 2.1.150): a note recorded read and published delivered that no request carried (`claudeNotesFloor`). A hook take that recorded its notes read as it answered left notes no session held recorded read (ADR-0067, "a hook's note is read only once its session shows it").
- **Tag-status:** unreleased


## The decision

**Every in-box runner whose harness can take words between its steps meets one contract: it takes, renders, bounds and confirms its notes, and says so with one optional capability.** What a note, a late call's item, a wake and another run's message are, how a serve becomes a read, the serve bounds, a lost take's settle and an ending's outcomes are ADR-0067's, ADR-0167's and ADR-0176's; this record states only what a runner owes them.

### 1. The capability
- `NoteInstruction() string` on the harness (`sandbox/executor/detached.go:noteReader`) makes `Runner.ReadsNotes()` true. Optional; the names stay. **A runner implements it only together with a confirm path** (section 3).
- A harness with no channel between its steps, or no way to confirm, declares no noteReader: its notes reach its review, its wakes run as turns of their own, and the #1818 coordinator approves that before it merges.
- Declared, `WriteConfig` appends the instruction last where `req.Steer.Set()` (`withNoteInstruction`), only in a box whose release can take notes (`claudecode.go:claudeNotesGate`). `warnNotesUnasked` (`sandbox/coordinator.go`) logs `steer_reader_never_ran` for a successful run that never asked, so the harness asks at least once in every successful run. With #1758 piece 7, only such a run receives `message_run` (`sandbox/runs.go:Coordinator.ReadsNotes`).

### 2. The take
One of two channels, each behind the run's own token (`api/mcpbridge/steer.go`):
- **Hook.** After each batch of the main agent's tool calls, every call answered, and as it is about to finish, never in a subagent's loop, the harness calls `steer_take` (never listed) on `SteerWiring.Server` over the run's bridge connection, naming the step and the agent; a subagent's or an unsubstituted agent gets nothing. **The runner renders the answer** (`hookDelivery.hookAnswer`) in its harness's own event names and JSON from the items and take the engine hands it: always text, empty for nothing, never an error nor a decision; its context before the model's next request; at the finish the turn goes on; nothing naming the take reaches the model. A 503 or any failure is gone past, the connection kept. The hook waits 10 s (`ClaudeHookTimeout`) for an answer due in 5 s (`steerTakeBudget`); a harness giving up sends `notifications/cancelled`.
- **Poll.** While the main session executes, `GET <URL>/notes?after=<seq>` (held 10 s, `steerHold`) answers one message an item. The harness admits each under its `inbox` id, so a repeat admits once, advances its cursor only past what it admitted, backs off on 503 (1 s to 30 s) and stops on 401 or 404 (`opencode_steer.js`).

**Each runner states its own ceiling**, measured as its harness counts, with the reason at the constant; Claude's 9,000 UTF-16 units (past 10,000 the model sees a 2 KB preview) and its item fitting move into Claude's hookDelivery. **A ceiling must hold the engine's largest single item beside a widest note**, or that item never reaches the runner. Those are the shared bounds: a note of 2,000 characters (`agent/steer.MaxNoteRunes`) from a 64-character sender (`iam.MaxLogin`), 4,493 units at two a character (`engine/lateitem.go:widestNote`); and `maxLateItemUnits`, 4,505, to which a late call's account (`lateItemMessage`), a wake item of up to 4 wakes of 1,000 characters (`sandbox/rowbytes.go:MaxItemWakes`, `steer.MaxWakeRunes`, held by `engine/wakeitem.go:wakeItemMessage`) and #1758's message from another run (pieces 7 and 8: a run of the seat or of another seat on the same work), one wake, are all held. So a hook answer's ceiling is at least 4,493 + 2 + 4,505 = 9,000 units, a poll message's 4,505. `maxLateItemUnits` and the widest note are the engine's own constants, never a runner's figure; Claude's 9,000 holds them with nothing to spare. A harness that measures lower comes to the coordinator: the fix lowers the shared bounds for every runner, never cuts an item for one.

### 3. Serve, then confirm
A served item is read only once confirmed (ADR-0067). Confirmation is a runner capability (`sandbox.ThreadTraits.Receipts`), read by `sandbox/mainthread.go:asksTakeReceipts` and `asksMainThread`, never by the harness's name, by exactly one of three cases (`confirms`, held by `internal/sandbox/executor.TestEveryHarnessThatReadsNotesCanConfirmATake`, which fails a note reader with none):
- **Hook receipts.** The session keeps the take's token in a record never sent to the model, beside a record that the same firing's context entered the conversation a resume hands back; the runner's `ReadMainThread` (`mainThreadReader`) answers `TakeReceipts` from them at every read of the job's box: held, restarted, absent only where the whole thread proves it, else undecided (executor package doc, "A hook's note, confirmed off its job's main thread"). How many takes one read asks (`maxAskedTakes`, 64 for Claude's masks) is the runner's.
- **Poll, by its session record.** A poll runner whose harness records the notes it admitted (`recordConfirmer`: Copilot's events.jsonl, Gemini's session file, Qwen's user record, Codex's rollout) answers the same `TakeReceipts` from that record, each take keyed by its inbox id, under which the poll recorded its serve beside the digest of the item's rendered text (`sandbox.NoteServe.Inbox`, `executor.ItemDigest`, never the text: ADR-0067); a runner matches a take by its inbox id or by that digest, whichever its record holds, through the one receipts path (`settleReceipts`): held only where the record holds it whole, a torn line never; a record that cannot be read undecided, with why, never not held.
- **Poll, by its plugin.** The harness's own view of each model request (`pluginConfirms`, OpenCode's `context` hook): its plugin posts to `<URL>/read` (1 to 24) the ids of the notes a request carries plain, again on 503, and after the run withdraws what was admitted and never delivered (`opencode.go:openCodeLeftoverSteers`).

**Confirmed means the model received the item as a current instruction**, never only inside a compaction summary: every harness compacts, and one that folds an admitted item into a summary framed as history (OpenCode's checkpoint) has not delivered it. Each case counts only a plain delivery: a record confirm holds no item found only in a summary, and a plugin confirms no item a compaction folded.

**A take is held where the session holds its item plainly in the conversation a resume hands back**, never where only a summary holds it. A later reply of the model is the required proof wherever the harness's resume has not been measured to hand back a trailing item no reply followed; where it was measured to, the record alone is enough.

A record read before the harness flushed its record decides nothing, never not held, and the settle at the run's end re-reads the record, so a wake or a run's message, served once (`MaxWakeServes`), is settled off what the run's end leaves rather than run as a turn of its own. A poll runner with none of the three never confirms: its notes wait for the review and its wakes run as turns of their own.

### 4. Rendering
The engine renders every item; the runner hands each message over whole, in order, as context, never as tool output. A person's note opens `## A note from <name>, sent while this turn was running`; a late call `## A call the run that made it stopped waiting for`; a wake, and with piece 7 another run's message, `## A message from <writer> about this work, sent while this turn was running`, its words in the ADR-0040 fence. Nothing precedes a heading on its line but framing `executor/transcript.go:headingFraming` knows: a harness that frames otherwise adds its framing there. A heading inside a fence heads nothing (`fenceEnd`): a transcript reader classifies by `headingAt`, never by a match of its own.

**The instruction's text lives once**, in `executor/steer.go`: `steerInstructionHead`, `steerInstructionTail` and the headings (`noteHeadOpen`, `noteHeadClose`, `lateCallHead`, `wakeHeadOpen`, `wakeHeadClose`), held equal to `internal/agent/prompts`' by test. A runner's instruction is the head, one sentence of its own saying where its harness puts the text, as measured, and the tail.

### 5. Continuation
What a continued session is told again is the engine's (`engine/runsteer.go:retoldNotes`, `sandbox/session.go:notesHeld`): a session that may not show what it read is told it again, as messages it may already show. The runner owes it the truth: whether its harness ended its own run, and no proof of holdings on a release whose resume was not measured (`claudeResumeRead`).

### 6. Brief
Notes waiting at launch reach the run in its brief (`LaunchRequest.BriefNotes`). The runner adds nothing, and takes nothing before the brief is read.

### 7. Tests
Each test in Enforced-by becomes a table over `Registered()`. A new runner adds its case and fixture, measured on its harness's real release, to each: the instruction is given exactly when the run can read and quotes every heading; its placement; its real config against a real bridge answers the main boundaries, not a subagent's; it confirms, and confirms nothing a compaction left only in its summary; its ceiling holds the shared bounds; its transcript files each item by its heading.

## Why the obvious alternative is wrong

**Each runner's own words for a note**: the transcript reader, the instruction and the confirm path read headings the engine writes once, so a runner's own heading files an item as model text. **A read recorded at the answer** was tried (Cost-when-tried). **A reader that cannot confirm** leaves every item served: its notes are told again and its wakes run twice (ADR-0167). **A per-runner item cut** would split #1758's items by harness.

## What this does not decide

Which channel each harness gets; how items are rendered, bounded and reported (ADR-0067, ADR-0167, ADR-0176).
